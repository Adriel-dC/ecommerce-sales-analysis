# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The e-commerce dataset contains 10,000 cleaned orders covering the period from January 2024 to December 2025.

The business generated $1.61 million in revenue and $537.12 thousand in profit, resulting in an overall profit margin of 33.27%.

## Key Findings

### 1. Home is the largest revenue category

Home generated $648,072.15 in revenue and $219,897.66 in profit, making it the strongest category in terms of total financial contribution.

### 2. Fitness has the highest profit margin

Fitness achieved the highest category-level profit margin at 34.51%, despite generating less revenue than Home.

### 3. Europe is the largest market

Europe generated $810,434.86 in revenue across 5,047 orders, making it the company's largest regional market.

### 4. Oceania has the highest regional margin

Oceania generated a 33.81% profit margin, slightly higher than Europe and North America.

### 5. Discounting is associated with unprofitable orders

There were 916 orders with negative profit.

These orders had an average discount of 10.28%, compared with 6.87% for profitable orders.

This suggests a potential relationship between higher discounts and order-level profitability and should be investigated further.

## Recommendations

- Investigate discount policies, particularly for orders with negative profit.
- Analyze low-margin products to identify opportunities for pricing or cost optimization.
- Investigate the Electronics category because it has the lowest category-level profit margin.
- Explore opportunities to expand sales in Oceania while maintaining its relatively strong profitability.
- Continue monitoring Home because it is the largest contributor to revenue and profit.
